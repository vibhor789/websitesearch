## 1. Business Models

### Model A: Pay-Per-Use (Recommended for Start)

**How it works:**
- Users pay for each website analyzed
- You charge markup on API costs

**Your Costs:**
- OpenAI: ~$0.003-0.012 per site
- ScraperAPI: FREE (5000/month)
- VPS: ~$12/month

**What You Charge:**
- $0.05-0.10 per website
- Markup: 300-800%

**Example:**
- User analyzes 100 websites
- Your cost: $1.20
- You charge: $5-10
- **Your profit: $3.80-8.80 (76-88% margin)**

## 2. Pricing Strategy

### Cost Breakdown (Your Expenses)

| Component | Cost | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| OpenAI API | $0.003-0.012/site | Depends on screenshots |
| ScraperAPI | FREE | 5000 requests/month |
| VPS Hosting | $12/month | Fixed cost |
| Domain | $1/month | ~$12/year |
| SSL | FREE | Let's Encrypt |

**Total Variable Cost: ~$0.005-0.015 per website**

### What to Charge (Market Rates)

Research shows similar tools charge:
- Clearbit: $0.10-0.25 per lookup
- Hunter.io: $0.04-0.10 per email
- LeadIQ: $0.15-0.30 per lead

**My Recommendation:**
- **Start at $0.05/website** (safe, competitive)
- Increase to $0.10/website after 10 customers
- Test $0.15/website for premium features

### Profit Margins

| Your Price | Your Cost | Profit | Margin |
|------------|-----------|--------|--------|
| $0.05/site | $0.01/site | $0.04 | 80% |
| $0.10/site | $0.01/site | $0.09 | 90% |
| $0.15/site | $0.01/site | $0.14 | 93% |

**Higher screenshots cost = Charge more for "Premium Analysis"**

## 3. Setting Up Payments

### Option A: Stripe (Recommended)

**Setup:**
1. Go to: https://stripe.com
2. Sign up for account
3. Verify your identity
4. Get API keys

**Integration:**
```bash
pip install stripe
```

**Pricing page with Stripe:**
- Checkout Sessions for one-time payments
- Subscriptions for recurring billing
- Easy setup, 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction

**Benefits:**
- Trusted globally
- Easy integration
- Handles tax calculation
- Multiple currencies
- Fraud protection

### Option B: PayPal

**Best for:**
- International customers
- Quick setup
- Lower trust barrier

**Fees:** 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction

### Option C: Paddle

**Best for:**
- Handling global taxes automatically
- SaaS businesses
- Higher fees but less hassle

**Fees:** 5-10% per transaction

### Credit System (My Recommendation)

1. Users buy credits: $10 = 200 credits
2. Each search uses credits based on settings
3. Credits never expire
4. Easy to understand
5. Encourages bulk purchases

## 6. Sales Techniques

### The Demo Script

1. **Hook (30 seconds)**
   "Let me show you something. What industry does your ideal customer work in?"

2. **Live Demo (2 minutes)**
   Run a search in front of them. Show real results.

3. **Value (1 minute)**
   "These 10 businesses don't have chat support. Each is a potential $500/year customer for you. That's $5,000 in potential revenue from 2 minutes of work."

4. **Close (30 seconds)**
   "Want me to set you up with 100 leads in your target market?"

### Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive"**
- "One customer pays for months of service"
- "How many hours do you spend prospecting? At $50/hour, this pays for itself in 30 minutes"

**"I can do this manually"**
- "How many sites can you check per hour? 10-20? Our tool checks 100 in minutes"
- "Your time is worth more than manual research"

**"How accurate is it?"**
- "90-95% accuracy with AI vision"
- "Here's a free sample—verify it yourself"

## 7. Legal Requirements

### Must Have:

1. **Terms of Service**
   - User responsibilities
   - Payment terms
   - Data usage
   - Disclaimers

2. **Privacy Policy**
   - What data you collect
   - How you use it
   - GDPR compliance
   - Data retention

3. **Refund Policy**
   - "Credits are non-refundable"
   - "Contact support within 24 hours for issues"

### Recommended:

4. **Business Registration**
   - LLC or Corporation
   - Protects personal assets
   - Tax benefits

5. **Business Insurance**
   - General liability
   - Cyber liability

### Important Legal Notes:

- Users are responsible for their API keys
- You don't store sensitive data
- Respect robots.txt and ToS of sites
- Comply with anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR)

## Key Metrics to Track

1. **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**
   - How much to get one customer?
   - Target: Less than 3 months of their payment

2. **Lifetime Value (LTV)**
   - How much does average customer pay total?
   - Target: 3x CAC minimum

3. **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**
   - Predictable monthly income
   - Goal: Grow 10-20% per month

4. **Churn Rate**
   - % of customers who cancel
   - Target: Less than 5% per month

5. **Profit Margin**
   - Revenue - Expenses
   - Target: 70%+ for SaaS
